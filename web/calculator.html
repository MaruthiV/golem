Where these numbers come from

Both efficiency figures are measured on golem, a 6.36M-parameter model on a $35 Tang Nano 20K, running through a real SDR SDRAM protocol in simulation. Reading one word at a time uses 8.2% of the bus. Full page bursts, with a page-aligned line buffer and ping-pong prefetch, use 93.8%. That is 12.13 cycles per weight word down to 1.07. Using the second number for your own design assumes you go and build all three of those things.

At the defaults, memory, empty context comes out at 15.3 tok/s. golem's own measured figure is 15.25. The fabric estimate just scales one measured design by width, so treat it as give or take half. Place and route is the only real answer, and past 90% of a part's LUT4 nextpnr will not place at all. 88% places, 95% does not, both measured.

Throughput here is derived, not observed. It is cycles divided by a clock, and none of it has run on hardware yet. Schedule efficiency is also not the same thing as a share of rated bandwidth. The two only line up when the memory runs at the clock it is rated for.
